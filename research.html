Attentional Drift Diffusion Model (2026)

I am developing a computational model of attentional decision-making that combines drift-diffusion dynamics with neural indicators of attention. The goal is to capture how people accumulate evidence over time when deciding where to direct attention under uncertainty, with a focus on linking cognitive theory to measurable behavioral and neural signals.
